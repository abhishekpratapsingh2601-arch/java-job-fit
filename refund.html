Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

What You Are Buying

JavaJobFit sells a single one-time digital product: the Pro Report for one resume scan, priced at ₹89 (launch offer, regular price ₹299). There is no subscription, no recurring charge, and nothing to cancel later. Payment unlocks the full report for that one scan immediately.

Refunds

Because the Pro Report is generated and delivered instantly at the moment of payment, it cannot be returned, and purchases are generally non-refundable once the report has been unlocked.

We will, however, refund you in full in any of these cases:

  • You were charged more than once for the same report. We refund every duplicate charge.
  • Your payment succeeded but the report did not unlock. Tell us and we will either unlock it or refund you — your choice.
  • A technical failure on our side left you without the report you paid for, for example the report could not be generated or was not viewable.
  • You were charged in error for something you did not attempt to buy.

We do not offer refunds because the score was lower than expected, because the suggestions were not what you hoped for, or because a job application was unsuccessful. JavaJobFit gives an ATS-style estimate and improvement suggestions; it does not promise interviews or offers, as set out in our Terms. The free scan shows you the score, the top gaps and the format of the report before you pay, so you can judge whether the full report is worth ₹89 to you.

Cancellation

There is nothing to cancel. This is a one-time purchase, not a plan, so no future payments are ever taken and no cancellation is required.
