Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

What You Are Buying

JavaJobFit sells two one-time products. Neither is a subscription, neither has a recurring charge, and there is nothing to cancel later.

  • Pro Report — ₹89 (launch offer, regular price ₹299). Payment unlocks the full automated report for one resume scan immediately.
  • Human Resume Review — ₹999. A person reviews your resume and emails the review within 3 days.

Pro Report Refunds

Because the Pro Report is generated and delivered instantly at the moment of payment, it cannot be returned, and purchases are generally non-refundable once the report has been unlocked.

We will, however, refund you in full in any of these cases:

  • You were charged more than once for the same report. We refund every duplicate charge.
  • Your payment succeeded but the report did not unlock. Tell us and we will either unlock it or refund you — your choice.
  • A technical failure on our side left you without the report you paid for, for example the report could not be generated or was not viewable.
  • You were charged in error for something you did not attempt to buy.

We do not offer refunds because the score was lower than expected, because the suggestions were not what you hoped for, or because a job application was unsuccessful. JavaJobFit gives an ATS-style estimate and improvement suggestions; it does not promise interviews or offers, as set out in our Terms. The free scan shows you the score, the top gaps and the format of the report before you pay, so you can judge whether the full report is worth ₹89 to you.

Human Resume Review Refunds

The review is delivered by a person, so its refund rules are different:

  • Before we start your review, you can cancel for a full refund for any reason.
  • If the review is not delivered within 3 days of both your payment and your resume email reaching us, you get a full refund.
  • Duplicate or mistaken payments are always refunded in full.
  • Once the review has been delivered, it is non-refundable.

Cancellation

There is nothing to cancel. This is a one-time purchase, not a plan, so no future payments are ever taken and no cancellation is required.

How to Request a Refund

Email javajobfit@gmail.com with the subject line Refund request and include:

  • the email address or UPI reference used for the payment,
  • the payment date and amount, and
  • your report link or report ID, if you have it, or the UPI transaction ID for a review.

Please do not include your resume text, job description, card details, UPI PIN, passwords, or any other sensitive information in a refund email — we do not need any of it to process a refund.

Timelines

We respond to refund requests within 3 business days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days, after which the time to appear on your statement depends on your bank or card issuer.

Payments and Currency

Pro Report payments are processed by Razorpay. Human review payments are made by UPI directly to the founder's account. JavaJobFit never sees or stores your card number, UPI PIN, or bank credentials. Prices are shown in Indian Rupees (₹) and charged in INR.

Questions

Anything unclear here, ask before you pay: javajobfit@gmail.com. See also our Terms and Privacy Policy.
